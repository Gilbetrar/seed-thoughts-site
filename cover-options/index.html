Cover Ideas for Joan

Here are eight ideas for a new cover, all made with your title, subtitle, and name. None of them is finished — think of them as sketches. Any color, picture, or lettering can be changed, and it is easy to make more from a new idea of yours.

How to choose: look through the covers below, then reply to Ben’s email with the number of the one or two you like best. You can also say what you like about one and dislike about another — that helps even more. Click any cover to see it larger. Under each one is a button that saves the full-size, print-quality file to your computer or phone.

About the files. Each “Download print-quality file” button saves a large JPEG (3,392 × 5,056 pixels at 300 dots per inch, about 8–10 MB). That is big enough to print as the front cover of a 6 × 9 inch book with room to spare, and it is the size Amazon and other printers ask for.

When you have chosen a favorite, Ben will prepare the final files a printer needs (the front, spine, and back together, sized to the page count) and can adjust the lettering or any detail before then. The web versions on this page are smaller copies for quick viewing.

Don’t see quite the right thing? Tell Ben what you picture — a scene, a feeling, a color — and he can have new ideas made in a day or two.
